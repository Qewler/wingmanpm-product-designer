# Surface: Renewable Operations Home

- Mode: operate
- Motion role: repeated product interaction

## User task

See current renewable assets, find a work order, understand its state, and take
the next safe action.

## Preserved authority

Record routes, business meaning, existing components, and protected copy before
implementation.

## Responsive decisions

- 390: compact top bar, task-first content, table-to-row-detail fallback.
- 768: keep navigation compact and expose filters without crowding.
- 1280/1440: persistent navigation, dense data, bounded reading regions.

## State decisions

Loading, empty, partial, error, success, disabled, permission, offline, and
responsive states are required for every applicable workflow.

## Review evidence

Record screenshots, keyboard checks, axe output, reduced-motion behavior, and
reviewer approval in `.wingmanpm-design/review.json`.
