# Surface: Secondary SaaS Marketing

- Mode: persuade
- Motion role: one rare explanatory focal sequence is allowed

## Product truth

Use the approved audience, product capability, proof, price, and next action.
Do not invent logos, customer quotes, metrics, or guarantees.

## First viewport

Show what the product is, who it serves, one primary action, and one product-
specific proof or mechanism. Keep the action usable at 390 and 1440 pixels.

## Motion

One focal explanatory sequence is allowed when it teaches mechanism or cause
and effect. The final state is readable without JavaScript. Reduced motion
removes scroll pinning, parallax, 3D transforms, and autoplay.

## Required states

Pending, error, and success behavior is required for signup, login, pricing,
upgrade, and contact actions used by the surface.
