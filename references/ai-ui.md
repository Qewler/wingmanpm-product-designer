# Transparent AI Interfaces

Use `ai-flow` or `ai-ui` for generation, assistants, retrieval, classification,
automation, and agent workflows. Preserve source boundaries, permissions,
business meaning, and human authority. Never imply certainty the system does
not have.

## Before execution

- Show the goal, inputs, included sources, excluded or missing context, and who
  can see the result.
- Let the user review editable instructions and remove unintended context.
- State whether the action creates a draft, changes records, contacts people,
  spends money, or triggers another system.
- Require explicit human approval before sending, publishing, deleting,
  purchasing, or making another consequential change.

## During execution

- Show meaningful stages or determinate progress when the system knows them.
  Do not invent percentages.
- Keep cancellation available for long work and explain what cancellation can
  and cannot undo.
- Stream content only when partial output helps. Keep layout stable and announce
  progress without flooding assistive technology.
- Distinguish queued, working, waiting for input, retrying, cancelled, partial,
  failed, and complete states.

## Results and recovery

- Put citations and provenance next to the claims or fields they support.
- Separate source facts, model inference, and user-authored changes.
- Show uncertainty, conflicts, missing context, and unsupported fields plainly.
- Make generated output reviewable and editable before consequential use.
- Preserve the user's edits across retry. A retry must state what input or
  strategy changed.
- For partial success, identify completed and failed items and offer scoped
  recovery instead of rerunning everything.

## Four axes

AI UI can raise Warmth through clear guidance and recovery, but it must not use
personality to hide uncertainty. Expression belongs in transparent process and
crafted result presentation. Repeated progress Motion stays restrained. Density
can increase for expert source review as long as provenance remains legible.

## Verification

Test slow, cancelled, partial, failed, offline, permission, stale-source,
conflicting-source, empty-result, and approval paths. Verify keyboard control,
focus after completion and failure, reduced motion, status announcements,
source access, and that no consequential action occurs before approval.
